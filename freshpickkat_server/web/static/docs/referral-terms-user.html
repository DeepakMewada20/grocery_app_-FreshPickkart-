1. How the Referral Program Works

Invite your friends to join FreshPickKat! When your friend signs up using your referral code, they get a welcome coupon instantly. You earn FreshPoints when your friend uses that coupon on a delivered order.

  1. Share your referral code
    Tap the share button on the Invite & Earn screen to send your unique code via WhatsApp, SMS, or any app.
  2. Friend signs up
    Your friend installs FreshPickKat and enters your referral code during onboarding or in their profile.
  3. Friend places an order with the coupon
    Your friend places their first order and applies the WELCOME coupon at checkout. The coupon only works if the cart meets the minimum order amount.
  4. Coupon redemption check
    Our system verifies that the WELCOME coupon was actually used on this order. Reward is only triggered when the coupon is redeemed.
  5. Order gets delivered
    The order must reach DELIVERED status for the reward to process.
  6. Reward is evaluated
    Our system checks the order against qualification rules and fraud prevention measures.
  7. You earn FreshPoints
    If everything passes, FreshPoints are credited to your account.

2. Reward Approval Process

Every referral goes through a review process before rewards are issued. This protects the program from abuse and ensures fairness.

What happens after your friend orders?

  1. Coupon Redemption Check — Our system verifies the friend used the WELCOME coupon on their order. If they didn't, no reward is triggered.
  2. Qualification Check — The order must meet the minimum actual payment requirement and reach DELIVERED status.
  3. Fraud Evaluation — Our system checks for suspicious patterns (see section 5 below).
  4. Outcome — Based on the evaluation, the reward is either approved, held, or rejected.

3. Qualification Requirements

For a referral to qualify for a reward, all of these conditions must be met:

  • The invited friend must be a new user — someone who has never had a FreshPickKat account before.
  • The friend must use the WELCOME coupon on their qualifying order. If the coupon is not applied, the reward will not trigger.
  • The order total must meet the coupon's minimum order amount (e.g. ₹199). If the cart is below this amount, the coupon won't apply.
  • The friend's payment amount must meet the minimum actual payment requirement (if enabled).
  • The order must reach DELIVERED status.
  • Each new user can only be referred once — duplicate referrals are not allowed.
  • You cannot refer yourself. Self-referral is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate rejection.

4. Reward Reversal

FreshPickKat reserves the right to reverse any referral reward if:

  • Fraud or abuse is detected after the reward was issued
  • The qualifying order is cancelled, refunded, or returned after the reward was processed
  • The referral violates any of the program's terms and conditions

What happens when a reward is reversed?

  • The FreshPoints that were credited are deducted from your balance.
  • If you have already spent some of those points, only the remaining balance can be deducted. The outstanding amount is tracked and may be recovered from future point earnings.
  • The referral status changes from REWARDED to REVERSED.
  • You will see the reversal reason in your referral activity timeline.
Example: You earned 50 points for a referral. Later, the qualifying order was returned. Your reward gets reversed. If you have 20 points remaining, those 20 are deducted, and 30 points are marked as outstanding recovery.

5. Fraud Prevention & Scoring

To keep the program fair, every referral is evaluated by our fraud prevention system. The system looks for patterns that suggest abuse.

Rules that can cause immediate rejection (hard reject)

Same User ID
You cannot use your own referral code. Your own code applied to your own account = automatic rejection.
Same Phone Number
If the referrer and invitee have the same phone number on file, the referral is rejected.
Already Rewarded
If the invitee has already been rewarded for another referral, duplicate rewards are prevented.

Rules that can add fraud points (soft scoring)

Same Address
If you and your friend share the same delivery address across multiple orders, it raises a flag. First match: +20 points. Multiple matches: +40 to +70 points.
Same Payment Contact
If the same phone number or UPI ID is used for payments by both accounts, it adds +30 fraud points.
Same Payer Name
If orders from both accounts share the same payer name, it adds +20 fraud points.
Referral Velocity
If you refer too many people in a short time (e.g., 5+ in 24 hours), each extra referral adds fraud points.

What happens based on the total fraud score?

Total Score Outcome
0–39 ✅ Auto-Approved — Reward is processed immediately
40–69 ⏳ Held for review — Usually released after 72 hours automatically
70–89 ⏳ Held for review — Released after the safety hold period
90+ ❌ Rejected — Reward is not approved
Hard reject ❌ Immediately Rejected — Same UID, same phone, or self-referral

    1. The friend uses the WELCOME coupon on their order
    2. The order reaches DELIVERED status

7. Program Rules & Fair Use

  • One account per person. Creating multiple accounts to earn referral rewards is considered abuse and will result in rejection of all related referrals.
  • Self-referral is prohibited. Using your own referral code on another account you control will result in immediate rejection and may lead to account suspension.
  • Don't share your code on public deal websites. Referral codes are meant for personal sharing with friends and family. Public distribution may be flagged as abuse.
  • Qualifying orders must be genuine. Orders placed solely to earn referral rewards without genuine intent to purchase may be disqualified.
  • All decisions are final. FreshPickKat reserves the right to make final decisions on all referral rewards, including reversal of previously issued rewards.

8. Modification & Termination

FreshPickKat reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the referral program at any time without prior notice. Changes may include:

  • Changing the reward amount or type
  • Adding or removing qualification criteria
  • Changing fraud detection thresholds
  • Suspending the program temporarily or permanently

If the program is terminated, all pending and held rewards will be reviewed and processed or reversed at the company's discretion.
